World Clock - Privacy Policy

The World Clock plugin does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
data. It makes no network requests, has no analytics or telemetry, and needs no
account.

The only data it reads and writes is a local settings file on your Mac:
~/Library/Application Support/Logi/LogiPluginService/PluginData/WorldClock/settings.json,
which stores your display preferences (which lines each key shows, 12/24-hour
format, renamed cities). It never leaves your computer. The "World Clock
Settings" action opens this file in your default text editor.
