## Short description (one line)

Live world clocks on your MX Creative Keypad – one city per key, plus a dial to plan across time zones.

## Full description

World Clock turns your MX Creative Console keys into a live, always-on world clock.

**Features**
- **One city per key** – 24 world cities (Honolulu to Auckland, plus UTC) grouped by region in the action picker. Each key shows a country outline map with the city marked, updating every second.
- **Time Travel dial** – turn a dial to shift every clock forward or back in 15-minute steps (up to ±24 hours) and instantly answer "what time is it there when it's 3 PM here?". Shifted times are clearly marked, and a **Back to Now** key resets everything.
- **Motorsport locations** – an extra group of keys for the 23 Grand Prix venues of the 2026 season, showing local time with the circuit layout as the background.
- **Customizable** – choose which lines each key shows (location, date, time, day, time zone, seconds), switch between 12/24-hour time, hide the map, and rename cities. Open **World Clock Settings** from any key; changes apply instantly on save.
- **Private and offline** – no account, no network access, no data collection. Time zone and daylight-saving rules come from your Mac.

**How to use**
1. Install the plugin and open Logi Options+.
2. Drag city actions from the World Clock group onto your keys.
3. Assign Time Travel to a dial and Back to Now to a key.

**Requirements:** macOS, Logi Options+ with Logi Plugin Service, MX Creative Console.
